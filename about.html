About

Writing in the hours between.

J.A. Strating is a Dutch author who writes adult dark epic fantasy, with a particular love for darker worlds, dangerous creatures, large-scale conflicts and characters facing impossible odds.

His love of imaginative worlds began early, shaped by authors including J.R.R. Tolkien, David Eddings and Terry Brooks. Peter V. Brett, Brandon Sanderson and Pierce Brown are among his more recent inspirations.

As a father of three with a full-time job, he writes in the quiet hours left between family, work and everyday life. His stories explore survival, loyalty, hope, war, found family and the choices people make when there are no easy answers.

Fera: The Amberium Conspiracy is the opening novel in his adult dark epic fantasy series set in the kingdom of Orgwell.
