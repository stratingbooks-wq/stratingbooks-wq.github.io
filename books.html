Fera: The Amberium Conspiracy

In Orgwell, creatures called Fera are enslaved through Control, and their strength helps determine their master's place in society. Cast out of his guild, Brum searches for a new Fera. Meanwhile, guild mistress Isabella follows a trail of illegal Amberium crystals. Their paths lead them into the same conspiracy.
