The official blurb

Welcome to Orgwell.

In Orgwell, humans enslave creatures called Fera through Control and put them to work or send them into battle. The strength of a person's Fera determines their place in society as surely as birthright.

By birth, Brum should have been privileged. Instead, his limited Control has driven him ever further down Orgwell's social hierarchy. After his Fera dies and his guild casts him out, he joins a Fera-catching expedition to earn enough for a new one. In the wilderness, Scales—an unknown Fera with an impossible healing gift—chooses to follow him. Brum finds refuge with a man who understands what that choice means.

Isabella stands near the top of that same hierarchy. She is mistress of the Solutions Guild, yet she despises the system that placed her there. Her parents were murdered, and she has never stopped looking for those responsible. A trail of illegal Amberium crystals finally gives her something real to follow, even if it costs her title or her life.

Deep in the jungle, Isabella finds the fugitive sheltering Brum. He guards two secrets that could tear Orgwell's social order apart. Fera can choose whom they bond with, and Amberium can be made artificially. Wendel, a high-ranking member of the Shadowdwell, has finally tracked him down. Now Brum and Isabella face the Fera he has brought to finish the hunt.

For readers who enjoy

Morally gray characters, creature bonding, an oppressive class system, political conspiracy and a story told across multiple viewpoints. This is not romantasy.

Content note: adult themes, graphic violence, gore, torture, mutilation, body horror, enslavement and abuse of magical creatures.
